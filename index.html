Manu Upadhyaya

I am a postdoctoral researcher with joint affiliations at Inria Paris in the Sierra team, working with Adrien Taylor, and at the Center of Applied Mathematics (CMAP) at École Polytechnique in the SIMPAS team, working with Aymeric Dieuleveut. I completed my PhD at Lund University (2025), where I worked on systematic and computer-aided analyses and design of first-order methods for structured optimization and inclusion problems. My advisors were Pontus Giselsson and Sebastian Banert.

Before that, I received an MSc in engineering physics (2020), an MSc in finance (2020), and a BSc in mathematics (2015), all from Lund University. I have also spent time as a visiting student researcher at UC Berkeley (2017) and ETH Zürich (2018).
